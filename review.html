HOW THE REVIEW PANEL IS DESIGNED TO WORK

Five lenses. One accountable decision.

  1. Source checker: Can we find the original study and the specific evidence?
  2. Methods reviewer: Were the experiments and statistics appropriate?
  3. Skeptic: What competing explanations or negative studies exist?
  4. Safety reviewer: Are we mistaking laboratory results for proven human treatment?
  5. Learning reviewer: Can readers verify the idea with the right public research tool?

Current status: These AI review roles are defined, but independent multi-model execution is not connected yet. Public submissions and automated metadata gates are available; outside experts have been invited to volunteer, but no expert reviewer appointment or scientific endorsement is implied.

Important scientific boundary: AI agreement is not peer review. Automated checks can catch missing citations, incomplete review records and publication-status problems; they cannot independently prove a claim is true. The public GitHub review forms are not for confidential manuscripts, unpublished private research, or any patient data.

How review decisions become transparent

Every new approved story must include a public editorial review record and source-located claim checks. Stories making clinical interpretations require an additional independent expert-review record. Publication is never automatic solely because AI reviewers agree.
