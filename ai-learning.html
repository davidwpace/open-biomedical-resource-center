How to begin

  1. Pick an example below or open a discovery lesson.
  2. Replace the bracketed words with your own public research question or topic.
  3. Copy the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another assistant you use.
  4. Ask for the source link, what the evidence actually shows, and what remains uncertain. Open the original source yourself.

Privacy: Do not paste patient records, personal medical histories, test results, confidential unpublished research, or credentials into public prompts. This website sends no prompt to any AI service.
