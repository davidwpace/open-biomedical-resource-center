03 / OUR PRINCIPLES

Accessible knowledge.
Evidence without shortcuts.

Open Biomedical Resource Center helps people spend less time hunting for research tools and more time exploring meaningful questions. It's a public directory—not a medical device, clinical decision-support system, or substitute for specialist interpretation. Every listing links to its official provider and reports known limitations.

A founding aspiration: Healing at the Pace of AI—while keeping evidence and people first.
