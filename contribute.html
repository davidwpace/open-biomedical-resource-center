HOW IT WORKS

One checkable contribution at a time.

  1. Find something public. A research article, dataset, or official resource page.
  2. Tell us what you noticed. Add a source and explain how to check it.
  3. Reviewers verify it. A submission isn't automatically an accepted scientific fact.
  4. Improve what everyone can learn. Valid corrections and references strengthen the center.
